How to play

As a Doll

  • Wind all five music boxes. Hold E beside one. Each finished box rings through the house.
  • When all five sing, the front door unlocks. Hold E to open it and escape.
  • Mend injured sisters with E. Revive downed ones before the Maker returns.
  • Hide inside toy chests when you hear him close. Hold E to climb in or out.
  • Q — Doll's Grace: a silent burst of speed. Shift — sprint, but he can hear it.

As the Dollmaker

  • Two strikes break a doll. A third shatters her where she crawls.
  • A finished music box reveals itself to you. So does a sprinting doll.
  • Q — Thread Sense: hear every doll through the walls for a few seconds.
  • Guard the front door when the fifth box begins to sing.
  • Left click / Strike to swing. A strike that misses is a long, loud second.
